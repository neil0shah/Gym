Manage Exercises

Different names/machines for the same movement (e.g. "Flat chest press" and "Barbell bench press") split your progress trends across names. Tag them as the same Exercise Variation here so charts on the Progress page combine them into one continuous trend — keep genuinely different variations (e.g. Preacher Curl vs. Dumbbell Bicep Curl) as separate variations even if they train the same muscle, since their numbers aren't comparable. This doesn't change any saved data — it only changes how exercises are grouped for charting. Muscle Group (below, on each exercise) is a separate, informal tag — e.g. "Biceps" — for your own reference; it doesn't affect any chart today.
